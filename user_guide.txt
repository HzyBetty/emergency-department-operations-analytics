 ED Flow Intelligence — User Guide
 Emergency Department Process Mining & Analytics Platform
===========================================================

1. OVERVIEW
-----------
This platform converts raw ED event logs into four categories
of operational insight, accessible through a four-tab dashboard:

  Tab 1 · Executive Overview    — KPI scorecards and system status
  Tab 2 · Patient Flow          — Sankey pathway map and bottleneck analysis
  Tab 3 · Risk & Admission      — ML-based admission prediction and zone analysis
  Tab 4 · Capacity Planning     — Hourly heatmap, scenario simulation, anomaly alerts


2. INSTALLATION
---------------
Requirements: Python 3.9 or higher

  Step 1 — Create and activate a virtual environment (recommended):
    python -m venv venv
    venv\Scripts\activate           # Windows
    source venv/bin/activate        # macOS / Linux

  Step 2 — Install dependencies:
    pip install -r requirements.txt

  Step 3 — Launch the app:
    streamlit run app.py

  The app opens automatically in your browser at http://localhost:8501


3. DATA REQUIREMENTS
---------------------
The app auto-loads the bundled file in the /data folder.
To use your own data, upload it via the sidebar.

Required CSV columns:
  visit_id          Unique ED visit identifier
  patient_id        Patient identifier
  timestamp         Event datetime (YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS)
  event             Clinical step name (e.g., Triage, Assessment, Discharge)
  triage_code       Acuity level (1 = most urgent, 5 = least urgent)
  initial_zone      Physical ED zone assigned at arrival
  disposition_code  Outcome code (code 7 = inpatient admission)
  disposition_desc  Outcome description text
  age               Patient age (integer)
  gender            Patient gender

Standard event names expected by the pathway diagram:
  Ambulance Arrival, Ambulance Transfer, Triage, Registration,
  Assessment, Consult Request, Consult Arrival, Discharge, Left ED


4. HOW TO USE EACH TAB
------------------------

TAB 1 — EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
  · The status banner at the top shows OPTIMAL / ELEVATED / CRITICAL
    based on median Length of Stay (LOS).
  · Use the five KPI cards to assess overall ED performance at a glance.
  · The LOS box plot shows variation within each triage level — wider
    boxes in L1/L2 indicate high-acuity bed-management risk.
  · The LWBS rate (Left Without Being Seen) highlights patient safety
    and revenue risk by triage level.

TAB 2 — PATIENT FLOW & BOTTLENECKS
  · Adjust the "Minimum pathway volume" slider to filter out rare
    transitions and focus on the main patient highway.
  · Red branches in the Sankey diagram indicate LWBS patients.
  · The bottleneck bar chart ranks transitions by average wait time.
    Use the "Full detail" expander to see 90th-percentile delays.
  · The Door-to-Triage chart compares performance against CTAS benchmarks.
    Red bars = accreditation gaps requiring targeted intervention.

TAB 3 — RISK & ADMISSION ANALYTICS
  · The feature importance chart shows which factors most predict admission.
  · AUC-ROC above 0.75 confirms the model is operationally useful.
  · Use the High-Risk Visit Flag List to initiate early bed booking for
    patients with predicted admission probability ≥ 70%.
  · Compare zone volume vs. zone median LOS to identify redeployment targets.

TAB 4 — CAPACITY PLANNING & ALERTS
  · The hourly heatmap highlights chronic delay windows.
    Horizontal bands = structural bottleneck.
    Diagonal clusters = shift-change handoff failure.
  · Use the efficiency slider in the simulation to model LOS targets
    and review the projected financial impact on the right panel.
  · The Red Flag section lists statistical outliers (Mean + 2 SD).
    Select a visit to view the step-by-step Root Cause Analysis timeline.


5. SIDEBAR FILTERS
-------------------
  · "Use bundled dataset" checkbox: auto-loads /data/event_log_ED_MMA_2026.csv.
    Uncheck to upload a custom CSV.
  · "Triage Level" multiselect: filter all tabs to a specific acuity group.
    Useful when presenting L1/L2 (high-acuity) analysis to clinical leadership.


6. TROUBLESHOOTING
-------------------
  · Blank screen after upload: Check that your CSV column names match
    exactly those listed in Section 3.
  · ML model warning: The model requires at least 50 visits with at
    least two distinct outcome values (admitted / not admitted).
  · "No transitions meet the minimum volume threshold": Lower the slider
    in Tab 2. This occurs when filtering to a small triage subset.
  · CTAS benchmark chart is empty: Ensure "Triage" events exist in the
    data and that triage_code values are numeric (1–5).
